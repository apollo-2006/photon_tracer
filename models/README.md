# Models

The models here come from elsewhere and keep their own terms; the project's MIT
license does not cover them.

| file | what | source and terms |
|---|---|---|
| `teapot.obj` | The Utah teapot, 6,320 triangles | Martin Newell, University of Utah, 1975, via Alec Jacobson's [common-3d-test-models](https://github.com/alecjacobson/common-3d-test-models) |
| `stanford-bunny.obj` | The Stanford bunny, 69,451 triangles | Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory, from the [Stanford 3D Scanning Repository](http://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/) (Greg Turk and Marc Levoy, *Zippered Polygon Meshes from Range Images*, SIGGRAPH 1994), via common-3d-test-models. Redistributed free of charge with credit, as the repository permits; not for commercial use without Stanford's permission. |
| `spot/` | Spot the cow, textured | Keenan Crane, from his [model repository](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/ModelRepository/); public domain. See `spot/README.md`. |

`fetch.sh` downloads more of the Stanford scans (the Armadillo, Happy Buddha and XYZ RGB
Dragon), which are not kept here because of their size: `models/fetch.sh armadillo`.
